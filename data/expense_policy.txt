Employees can claim travel expenses within 30 days of the trip. Flights must be economy class and booked at least 7 days before travel. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to 6000 rupees per night in metro cities and 4000 rupees elsewhere. Meals during travel are reimbursed up to 1500 rupees per day with itemised bills. Claims above 25000 rupees need approval from a director. Submit claims in the finance portal with scanned receipts. Reimbursements are paid in the next monthly payroll cycle. Personal expenses and alcohol are never reimbursed.
