Nimbus Labs offers 18 days of paid casual leave per calendar year. Sick leave is separate: 10 days per year, and a medical certificate is required for absences longer than 2 consecutive days. Unused casual leave can be carried forward up to 5 days into the next year, but sick leave cannot be carried forward. Maternity leave is 26 weeks and paternity leave is 10 working days. Leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 3 working days in advance, except for emergencies. Managers approve or reject requests within 2 working days. Public holidays are not counted as leave days.
