Employees receive a company laptop on their first day. To connect to the office Wi-Fi, choose the network NimbusSecure and sign in with your company email and password. If you forget your password, reset it at the self-service portal; the reset link expires after 15 minutes. VPN access is mandatory when working from home and uses the Nimbus VPN app with two-factor authentication. For hardware faults, raise a ticket at the helpdesk. Critical issues such as a dead laptop are answered within 4 hours, and normal issues within 2 working days. Never install unapproved software on company devices.
